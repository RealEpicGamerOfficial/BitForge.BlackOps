Bounty Protocol

Everything an operative needs to know.

What qualifies as a reportable bypass? ↓
Any method that allows a user to access restricted content, circumvent usage limits, or hide their activity from the BitForge administrative systems. This includes URL bypasses, mirror sites, and script injections.
How are payments issued? ↓
Once a bypass is verified and patched, we issue payments via the method specified in your report. Our primary method is Cash (USD). Payout amounts vary based on the severity of the vulnerability.
Can I remain anonymous? ↓
Yes. While we need contact info for payment, all report data is handled internally and your identity is not disclosed to the general BitForge user base. We value operative confidentiality.
How long does verification take? ↓
Standard reports are processed within 48 to 72 hours. High-priority "Critical" reports are usually addressed within 12 hours of submission.
